<!-- Puzzle format constraints from Phase 1. Every puzzle object must conform exactly. -->

From src/puzzles/loader.js (parsePuzzle rules):
- `schemaVersion: 1` — required on every entry
- `id: string` — unique, 8-char alphanumeric (use short random-looking IDs like `'a1b2c3d4'`)
- `title: string` — display name (e.g. `'Puzzle 1'` through `'Puzzle 40'`)
- `goalType: 'capture-all-targets' | 'reach-all-goal-squares'`
- `targetColor: string | null` — required when goalType is `capture-all-targets` (e.g. `'black'`); null for reach goal
- `grid: string[]` — array of strings, same length (width), each char is:
  - `'x'` = impassable (excluded from board Map entirely)
  - `'-'` = empty playable square
  - `'G'` = goal square (for reach puzzles; also playable/empty)
  - uppercase = white piece: `P` pawn, `R` rook, `B` bishop, `N` knight, `Q` queen
  - lowercase = black piece: `p` pawn, `r` rook, `b` bishop, `n` knight, `q` queen
  - **No kings (`K`/`k`) — ENG-04: king never appears in Xess**
- `pawnDirections` — required only when grid contains pawns (`P` or `p`). Object keyed by `'col,row'` (0-indexed) mapping to direction vector `[dc, dr]`:
  - `[0, -1]` = moves up (towards row 0)
  - `[0, 1]` = moves down (towards last row)
  - `[1, 0]`, `[-1, 0]` = horizontal
  - Every pawn in the grid must have a corresponding entry; missing entries will throw

Capture puzzle: all pieces matching `targetColor` must be captured. White pieces are the player's pieces.
Reach puzzle: all `G` cells must be occupied by a white piece. No targetColor.

Movement rules recap:
- Rooks/bishops/queens: slide along rays, blocked by any piece or board edge, cannot enter impassable
- Knights: jump (2+1), ignore blocking pieces, landing square must be playable and not occupied by own piece
- Pawns: move forward 1 in their direction, capture diagonally (90-degree rotation of direction vector)
