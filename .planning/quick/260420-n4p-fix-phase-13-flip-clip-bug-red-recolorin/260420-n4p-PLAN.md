<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Fix FLIP clip bug and red recoloring in board.css</name>
  <files>src/styles/board.css</files>
  <action>
Two independent edits to src/styles/board.css:

**Edit A — remove overflow:hidden from .cell**
In the `.cell` rule (lines 23–38), remove the line:
  `overflow: hidden;`
Keep `isolation: isolate` and every other property untouched. The `.cell::after` pseudo-element uses `inset: 0` with `border-radius: inherit` and does not need overflow clipping. The FLIP animation sets `transform: translate(dx, dy)` on the piece to position it at the source square, then transitions it to zero — with `overflow: hidden` the piece is invisible during that translate phase.

**Edit B — replace opponent piece filter**
Replace the existing filter on:
  `.board[data-goal-type='reach-all-goal-squares'] .piece[data-piece-key^='black-']:not(.piece--ghost)`

Current value (does nothing on achromatic SVGs):
  `filter: hue-rotate(-26deg) saturate(180%) brightness(0.82) contrast(1.16);`

Replace with:
  `filter: sepia(1) saturate(4) hue-rotate(320deg) brightness(0.75);`

The Cburnett SVG pieces are black/white — pure achromatic. `hue-rotate` alone shifts no hue. The sepia-first sequence injects brown hue, `saturate(4)` amplifies it, `hue-rotate(320deg)` rotates brown to crimson, `brightness(0.75)` deepens it. Do NOT touch `.board[data-goal-type='capture-all-targets']` or any other rule.
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>grep -n 'overflow: hidden' src/styles/board.css; grep -n 'sepia(1)' src/styles/board.css</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>
`grep overflow: hidden src/styles/board.css` returns no match in the .cell rule block.
`grep sepia src/styles/board.css` returns the new filter line.
`isolation: isolate` still present in .cell.
  </done>
</task>
